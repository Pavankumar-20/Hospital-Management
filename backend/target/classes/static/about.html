Our Story

Three Decades of Healing Excellence

Founded in 1995, MediCare Plus Hospital has grown from a small clinic to one of the region's most trusted healthcare institutions. Our mission has always been to provide accessible, affordable, and exceptional medical care to every patient who walks through our doors.

Today, we serve over 50,000 patients annually with a team of 150+ expert doctors across 25 specialized departments. Our commitment to innovation and patient-centered care has earned us JCI accreditation and numerous healthcare excellence awards.
